Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

How long does it take to get paid? ▼

Payments are processed instantly through eSewa and Khalti. You can withdraw to your bank account within 24 hours.

What are the fees? ▼

First Rs. 5,000 earned every month is completely free. After that, we charge 3-5% transaction fee. No hidden charges!

Can I change my coffee price? ▼

Yes! You can change your coffee price anytime from your dashboard. Your fans will see the updated price.

Is my data secure? ▼

Absolutely! We use industry-standard encryption and partner with trusted payment gateways. Your data is always secure.

Can I delete my account? ▼

Yes, you can delete your account anytime. We'll process any pending payments first, then remove all your data.
